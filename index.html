Train for WorldSkills Web Technologies.

Skill17 collects everything our competitors and experts use to prepare for EuroSkills and WorldSkills: the standards, the competition path, and ready-to-run project setups for every allowed framework.

The road to WorldSkills

  1. National selection

    Each country runs its own regional and national rounds. Winning earns a place on the national team.

  2. Continental competitions

    EuroSkills and other regional events are where top performers get competition experience at international level.

  3. WorldSkills International

    Every two years, competitors from over 60 countries meet for four competition days. You must be under 23 in the competition year.
